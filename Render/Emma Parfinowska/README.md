# Diary
# 06/10/2026 - Start of blender
- The fist sections on designing the model is starting whith a sphere then using the 'clay' and 'elastic' tool to change the shape of the body. My idea is to create the main shape of Mew then be able to rig the body and be able to pose with it. So after shaping the body i extended parts to make up the arms and legs. I then attached 3 cones to the end of the arms to create fingers for Mew. which look like claws. 


# 13/10/2026 - Building mew V1
- I have added in more detail for the legs and feet. i used spheres and sculpted them to fit the thighs and feet. For the tail i originlyy used a sphere and shaped and streched it but I didnt like the look of it. I then found out how to use the curve tool, so i drew mews tail and filled it in and placed a sphere on the tip of the tail as it will match mews design. I have also added on the head but it needs shaping as it doesnt quite match its design. Added in the ears for mew and made an attempt for the eyes. mews eyes are a smooth triangle so i need to smooth the current eyes now as it looks out of place as just pointy triangles however, i got placement for it will be placed.

# 15/10/2026 - Continuation of Mew V1
- I have decided to scrap the triangular eyes entirely ad decided to hollow out the face and fill it in with spheres for the eyes. I have also redone the tail as it was to small in comaprison to mew. I now need to shape the head to the eyes so it fits better as well as rendering the body as well. 

# 16/10/2026 - Mew V2
- I have decided to redo my model as it was misaligned and looked creepy. Not how mew was supposed to look like. with the current skill and tool i know i will be able to create a better looking model ofg mew. i have decided to start with the head. I started with a sphere and with the elastic tool and sculpting tools i was able to make the shape of the head. I also udsed the mirror tol too keep it symetrical. I inflated thew ears and hollowed out where the eys will be. 
- I have made a sphere and elongated it to make the shape of the body. i then used different scuplt tools to create the shape of the body. it is a bottom heavy build so the point of the neck is narrower than the bottom of the body. I also added in groves to where the arms will be placed and should make it easier to rigg/ animate mew for the future. 
- The arms i used a sphere and elongated it. i then sculpted out the tiny paws for mew and duplicated. one duplicated it i added it to the body and made the arms proportionate to mew body. I also added a screenshot of what the render looks like up until this point.
-Made the model smooth as well as fixed the proportians. 
- Added in the thighs and shaped it to mew. planning on doing the legs which are thin and long and joining them to the thighs
- Made the path and subdivided it to create the bends and kinks in mews tail. I then thickned the tail and attached it to the body. I then extruded the tail by one more section and expanded the radius to create the tip of mews tail. I also expanded the radius by the base of the tail to make the shape. 

# 25/10/2026 - Final Touches
- Created a new head that fits the shape better for the final design and made sure you can see the eyes from the side for this one. Added in progress of the final head that will be used in animation.
